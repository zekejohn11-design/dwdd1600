Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, computers, phones, and other digital technology safely, responsibly, and respectfully.

Digital law

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use computers, the internet, and digital technology.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a set of rules that outline how users should behave when using a computer, network, or other digital technology.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work or ideas without giving proper credit, presenting it as your own.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty refers to any form of cheating or plagiarism in an academic setting, including copying someone else's work, using unauthorized materials, or falsifying information.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI generated content must be used responsibly and ethically. Users should ensure that the content is accurate, does not infringe on intellectual property rights, and complies with relevant laws and regulations.

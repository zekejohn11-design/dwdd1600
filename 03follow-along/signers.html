Who Were The Signers

Famous Signers

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress and the first signer of the Declaration of Independence.
Thomas Jefferson
Primary author of the Declaration of Independence.
Benjamin Franklin
One of the most prominent figures of the American Revolution and a key negotiator of the Treaty of Paris.
John Adams
One of the leading advocates for independence and a key figure in the Continental Congress.
Samuel Adams
A leading figure in the American Revolution and a key organizer of the Sons of Liberty.

Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the United States Declaration of Independence were

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; famous for his large, prominent signature.
Benjamin Franklin
Renowned scientist, inventor, diplomat, and statesman.
Thomas Jefferson
Primary author of the Declaration and later the third U.S. president.
John Adams
Major advocate for independence and later the second U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Influential Massachusetts revolutionary and later governor of Massachusetts.
John Witherspoon
Minister, educator, and president of the College of New Jersey (now Princeton University).
George Walton
Georgia delegate and later governor of Georgia.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
The only Roman Catholic signer and one of the last surviving signers.

Fun fact: There were 56 signers in total, representing the 13 colonies. Not all of them signed on July 4, 1776—the signing process continued into August.
